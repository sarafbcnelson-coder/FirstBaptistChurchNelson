A note from Pastor Elena

We built this church for people who've been out of church.

Maybe you grew up in a congregation and drifted. Maybe you never set foot in one until now. Either way, you don't need to have it figured out before you walk through our doors.

We're a mid-sized, multigenerational community — kids running the halls, longtime members, and folks in their very first week. We read scripture honestly, sing without a fog machine, and try to leave room for real doubt.

— Pastor Elena Ruiz
